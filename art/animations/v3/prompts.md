# Identity and combo asset prompts / v3

Built-in image_gen generated all designs and articulated poses with transparent_background=true. Selected PNGs were copied into art/concepts/identity-v3/ and art/animations/v3/source/. The initial Do Mixi skills sheet was replaced with body poses and tiny equipment glows; large sound rings are drawn by the game's VFX code.

Intended dimensions in prompts are design hints only. Actual PNG dimensions and measured crops are recorded in each manifest.json. Technical packing uses nearest-neighbour, explicit pivots and true alpha; no pose is invented by code.
